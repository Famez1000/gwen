ANXIETY RELIEF, PANIC ATTACK HELP & A CALMER MIND

Gwyn is your personal companion for anxiety, panic attacks, overthinking, stress, and anxious thoughts. Whether you need help calming down in an overwhelming moment or want to work on your anxiety over time, Gwyn gives you practical tools and personalized guidance to help you move forward.

More than simply helping you relax, Gwyn follows a simple approach:

COPE. UNDERSTAND. HEAL.

First, learn how to cope when anxiety or panic strikes. Then explore the thoughts, feelings, and patterns behind your anxiety. Finally, create a personal plan with Gwyn to gradually build a calmer, healthier mindset.

STOP OVERTHINKING & BREAK THE ANXIETY CYCLE

Anxiety can create a cycle of worrying, negative thoughts, physical tension, and more anxiety. Gwyn helps you interrupt that cycle using calming exercises, grounding techniques, reflection, journaling, and supportive guidance.

Learn to notice anxious thoughts without letting them control you, challenge negative thinking patterns, and gradually replace them with healthier ways of responding.

YOUR PERSONAL ANXIETY PLAN

Gwyn can help you create a personal plan based on what you are experiencing and what you want to work on.

Your plan can focus on coping with anxiety, understanding your triggers and thought patterns, or taking steps toward longer-term emotional wellbeing.

PANIC ATTACK & ANXIETY RELIEF

When anxiety becomes overwhelming, Gwyn gives you simple tools you can use immediately. Slow your breathing, ground yourself in the present moment, redirect your attention, and give your mind and body time to settle.

BREATHING EXERCISES

Follow calming breathing exercises designed to slow your breathing, relax your body, reduce stress, and create space between you and anxious thoughts.

GROUNDING TECHNIQUES

Use sensory grounding exercises to reconnect with the present moment when anxiety, panic, fear, or racing thoughts feel overwhelming.

JOURNALING & REFLECTION

Write about your day, thoughts, worries, and feelings. Track your anxiety level and let Gwyn turn your journal entries into supportive reflections that can help you better understand yourself.

MOOD & ANXIETY TRACKING

Record how you feel and monitor your anxiety over time. Discover patterns, recognize improvements, and learn which activities help you feel calmer.

CALMING ACTIVITIES

Explore affirmations, gentle distractions, relaxation exercises, reflections, and soothing activities whenever you need to relax your mind or reset your thoughts.

REMINDERS

Create gentle reminders to breathe, check in with yourself, reflect, journal, or simply take a quiet moment during your day.

PROGRESS INSIGHTS

Review your breathing sessions, calming streaks, anxiety logs, reflections, and activities to see your progress and discover what works best for you.

CHAT WITH GWYN

Talk with Gwyn whenever you're feeling anxious, stressed, overwhelmed, or caught in overthinking. Gwyn provides supportive AI-powered guidance, grounding suggestions, practical exercises, and gentle encouragement.

A CALMER MIND, ONE STEP AT A TIME

Gwyn is designed to feel warm, simple, and human. It doesn't tell you to “just calm down.”

Instead, Gwyn guides you through small, manageable steps: breathe, ground yourself, notice your thoughts, reflect, understand what you're feeling, and decide what to do next.

Think of Gwyn as a pocket companion for anxiety relief, panic attack help, relaxation, self-reflection, and everyday emotional wellbeing—there when you need support and there when you're ready to work on yourself.

IMPORTANT

Gwyn is intended for general wellbeing and self-help purposes. Gwyn is not a medical device, therapist, crisis service, or replacement for professional medical or mental health care.

If you are in immediate danger, think you may harm yourself or someone else, or need urgent help, contact your local emergency services or a trusted person immediately.
